Approach to the Business

Owning a book of 20 named accounts — depth over volume. Every logo gets a plan, research, and multi-threaded coverage.

Full Portfolio Coverage

  • Own and grow all 20 accounts
  • Whitespace analysis: ITSM + AIOps + Digital Workplace on every logo
  • Account Plans with Executive Sponsor Mapping for the full book

Research Cadence

  • AI-assisted annual report & strategy review on all 20
  • Map KPIs, risks & initiatives to Helix value
  • Quarterly insight refresh + trigger monitoring

Pipeline Coverage

  • 2.5× pipe coverage to quarterly target
  • Stage-weighted forecast with evidence
  • Weekly review: qualified vs. coverage gaps across the 20

30 / 60 / 90 Day Plan

First 90 days focused on building internal relationships and external account momentum across the book of 20.

Days 1–30: Listen & Align

  • Internal: 1:1s with AE, SE, and manager and other supporting roles in the business. Understand what makes each person successful, their working style, and how they prefer to collaborate.
  • Map the internal team structure and key stakeholders (sales, pre-sales, customer success, partners).
  • Deep-dive on the existing 20-account book: current status, open opportunities, risks, and white space.
  • Start AI-assisted research on the full portfolio (annual reports, strategy, KPIs).

Days 31–60: Build & Engage

  • Internal: Align with SEs on technical positioning and with CS on expansion signals. Establish clear collaboration rhythms (joint account reviews, deal strategy sessions).
  • Complete account plans for all 20 logos with executive mapping and hypotheses.
  • Begin multi-threaded outreach on priority accounts using research-backed insights.
  • Identify and engage key partners that overlap with the 20 accounts.

Days 61–90: Generate & Forecast

  • Internal: Run first joint pipeline review with the broader team. Share what I've learned about account priorities and ask for input on collaboration improvements.
  • Establish 2.5× pipeline coverage discipline with stage-weighted evidence.
  • Advance 3–5 qualified opportunities with clear MEDDPICC progress.
  • Deliver first quarterly account insight summary to leadership and the team.

Account Research & Insight Engine

From annual reports and public strategy to BMC Helix value mapping — applied across the full book of 20 accounts.

1

AI + Annual Report Scan

Extract strategic priorities, KPIs (availability, cost, resilience, digital transformation), risks (operational, compliance, talent), and investment areas for each of the 20.

2

Map to Helix Portfolio

Link findings to ITSM (service quality, deflection), AIOps (noise reduction, MTTR, probable cause), Automation & Digital Workplace (productivity, self-service).

3

Hypothesis & Trigger

Build 2–3 evidence-based hypotheses per account. Identify external triggers (regulation, cloud migration, major incident, cost program) that create urgency.

4

Executive Narrative

Translate into a 1-page insight brief that connects their published goals to measurable Helix outcomes (e.g. 50–75% MTTR reduction, 90% event noise cut).

Successful Strategies

Patterns that consistently close inside a focused 20-account book.

Value-based Selling

Lead with the account’s published KPIs and risks. Quantify Helix impact (MTTR reduction, event noise cut, automation rate) and present a simple ROI model early.

Multi-Threading + Champion

Parallel engagement across IT Ops, Service Desk, and business inside each of the 20. Equip a champion who can advance the deal internally.

Land & Expand

Focused ITSM entry to prove value quickly, then expand into AIOps and Digital Workplace within the same logo. Primary growth lever in a 20-account book.

Competitive Displacement

Where ServiceNow or legacy environments show AIOps depth, TCO, or automation gaps. Use research to surface dissatisfaction early.
